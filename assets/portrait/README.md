- Render 280×280 with width and height in the markup; export at 560×560 (twice the render size).
- `radius-lg` (18px), 1px `color-border` hairline on a `color-surface` backing, `shadow-md`.
- WebP, preloaded, `fetchpriority="high"`; the preload URL and the img src must be identical, including `www`.
- Alt text: "Sergio Beleño - Technical Lead (VTEX) | Full Stack Web Developer" — the name and role line, not a description of the photograph.
- Head and shoulders, eyes on the upper third, even soft light, plain background dark enough to sit on #0f0f0f without a seam.
- One portrait across the site, LinkedIn and GitHub. No filters, vignettes or duotones; never on a light background.
