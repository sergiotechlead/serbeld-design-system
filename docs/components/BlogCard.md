# BlogCard

A post teaser that is one link: a 160px cover image, the title clamped to two lines in `card-title`, the excerpt clamped to three in Lekton 14px; 24px body padding, at least 360px tall. Hover strengthens the border and lifts the card 2px — the only card that moves.

**Provide:** `href`, `title`, `excerpt`, `image` with alt (or an empty alt when decorative), optional `date`. Lay out in `.sb-blog-grid`: three columns, two at 900px, one at 600px.

- Do: give every post its own 1200×630 Open Graph image and a link back to a relevant home section.
