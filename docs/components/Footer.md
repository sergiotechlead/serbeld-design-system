# Footer

The closing band: a 1px `color-border` top rule, 32px padding, the copyright in Lekton 13px `color-text-faint` and the social cluster at a 16px gap; at least 6.4375rem tall (8.75rem on mobile), stacking and centring at 560px.

**Provide:** optional `social` links and `children` to replace the default text. The default year comes from the current date, never a hardcoded value. Defer its content with a sentinel until it nears the viewport if the page is long.
