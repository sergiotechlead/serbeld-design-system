# TimelineCard

One entry per role or credential: a 38px logo, 16px gap, date in `meta`, the role in `card-title`, then company · location (mode) in Lekton 14px; 24px padding (16px at 560px), at least 96px tall.

**Provide:** `title`, `company`, `location` with the working mode in brackets, `date` as a year range, `logo` (square, requested at 38×38 WebP) and its alt as a full sentence. Only the newest entry gets `current`: the Current badge plus the `color-accent-shadow` border. Credentials may add `description` and `link` ("View credential", external icon).

- Do: run newest first; a promotion inside a company produces two entries.
- Don't: publish an entry with a missing logo or date, or leave two entries current.
